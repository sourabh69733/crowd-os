## Full description (max 4000)

Freegram is a public notice board that keeps working when the internet doesn't.

Post what's happening around you: where help is, which roads are closed, what's going on at the gate. When the network is down or overloaded, phones nearby pass posts to each other over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. As soon as any phone carrying a post gets back online, the post reaches the internet too.

WORKS WITHOUT INTERNET
• Turn on Nearby and Freegram swaps posts with Freegram phones around you, in the background.
• Posts travel from phone to phone, up to 6 hops.
• Made for crowded fests and stadiums, floods and power cuts, and places where the network fails.

POSTS CAN'T BE FAKED
• Every post is signed by its author's key. Nobody can change a post or post as someone else's ID.
• Names always show the end of the ID, so copied names stand out.

NO PHONE NUMBER, NO EMAIL
• Your ID is made on your phone. Nobody sees your number.
• Back up your ID with a password and restore it on any phone.

YOU CHOOSE WHO MODERATES
• Report posts privately to maintainers. Block or mute anyone.
• Maintainers can hide posts and people for those who follow them, and their decisions are public. You can switch any maintainer off.

SAFE BY DESIGN
• Photo location and camera details are removed automatically.
• Panic wipe erases everything on the phone with one long press.
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

FIND PEOPLE
• Discover recent posts and people suggested by maintainers.
• Follow by QR code, even offline.
• Reply to and like posts.

OPEN STANDARD
Freegram uses Nostr, an open network. Your posts can also be read in other Nostr apps.

Important: everything on Freegram is public. It is not a private messenger.
