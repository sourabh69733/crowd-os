# Freegram terms of use

Version 1, 7 October 2026. Contact: sourabhsahu69733@gmail.com

The app shows a short version of these terms during setup (users tap "I understand and agree") and in Settings → Rules and terms. Keep the in-app text (`shared/.../ui/Rules.kt`) in step with this page and with [PRINCIPLES.md](../PRINCIPLES.md).

## 1. Public by design

Everything you post, reply, like or set as your name is public and can be copied anywhere. Posts can't be recalled once shared.

## 2. Your responsibility

You are responsible for what you post. Follow the law where you live. You must be 18 or older.

## 3. Not allowed

- Child sexual abuse material. Zero tolerance.
- Fake content presented as real: doctored photos, invented events, false locations of help.
- Pretending to be someone else or an official body.
- Threats or calls for violence against people.
- Posting someone's address, phone number or ID documents, or exposing people to danger.
- Intimate images shared without consent.
- Hate that targets people for religion, caste, gender, ethnicity or similar.
- Scams, fraud and spam.

Real events, criticism of anyone, dark humour, strong opinions and hard truths are allowed.

## 4. Moderation

Maintainers you follow can hide posts and people for you. Their hide lists are public, and you can switch any maintainer off. New installs follow a default maintainer. Report a post from its ⋮ menu; reports go privately to your maintainers. You can block or mute anyone on your phone. Abusive content is acted on as soon as a maintainer sees a report, and repeat or severe offenders are hidden for everyone who follows that maintainer.

## 5. No account on our servers

Freegram has no company server and keeps no account for you. Your ID lives on your phone. If you lose it without a backup, nobody can restore it.

## 6. No warranty

Freegram is an early test version, provided as is, without guarantees of delivery, availability or safety. Don't rely on it alone in an emergency.

## 7. Changes

We may update these terms. The app shows the current version in Settings → Rules and terms.
