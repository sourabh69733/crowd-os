// what is freegram

A feed that doesn't need a tower.

Networks go down during floods, crowds, blackouts and outages. That's exactly when people need to share what's happening. Freegram is built for that moment.

Works offline

No internet? Posts hop between nearby phones over Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi, spreading through the crowd on their own.

Signed by you

Every post carries your own cryptographic signature. Anyone can pass it along, but nobody can quietly change it.

Reaches the world later

When any phone that carries your post finds a connection, it goes online automatically. Your voice gets out.

Photos, peer to peer

Pictures are shared by fingerprint, so any phone that has one can hand it to the next. No single server to block.

// what you can do

Everything a public board needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Post, reply, like

Share updates with an optional photo, answer posts, and react. Replies travel nearby too.

Discover people

See recent posts from everyone on Freegram, search by name or ID, and follow by scanning a QR code, even offline.

No phone number

Your ID is made on your phone. No email, no number, nobody added to random groups. Back it up with a password.

You choose who moderates

Report posts privately. Maintainers can hide posts for those who follow them, publicly. Switch any maintainer off.

Panic wipe

Hold one button for two seconds and everything on the phone is erased: your ID, posts, photos and who you follow.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics and nothing sent to us. Photos have their location removed before posting.

How to install

  1. Tap Download APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser.
  3. Tap Install, open Freegram, and follow the short setup.
  4. Make the encrypted backup it offers, so you never lose your ID.

Requirements

  1. Android 8 or newer.
  2. Nearby sharing needs Google Play services, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi.
  3. Internet is optional: posts wait and go out when any phone carrying them is back online.

Know before you post

  1. Everything is public. Anyone who gets a post can copy it anywhere. It's not a private messenger.
  2. Not anonymous. Your ID can be linked to you by what you post, and servers see your internet address.
  3. Early version. Don't rely on it alone in an emergency.
